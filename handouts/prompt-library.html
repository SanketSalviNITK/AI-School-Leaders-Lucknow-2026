Office & circulars · कार्यालय और परिपत्र

OF-01 Handwritten note to a bilingual circular · हस्तलिखित नोट से द्विभाषी परिपत्र
When: You have a rough note, a WhatsApp message or a photo of a notice and need a proper circular. · Practised in Session 1 · Mission 2
Turn my rough note below into a formal CBSE-style circular for [parents of Classes ___ / all staff].
Include: reference number [___], date [___], subject line, body, and signature block for the Principal.
Keep it under 180 words, in simple language. Give English first, then Hindi, with the same meaning.
My note: [paste or type the note here, or attach the photo]
End with "Please verify": every date, time, fee and venue I must check.
OF-02 Summarise a CBSE circular for staff · CBSE परिपत्र का स्टाफ के लिए सारांश
When: A new CBSE circular has arrived and you need staff to understand it in five minutes.
I am pasting a CBSE circular below. Summarise it for my teachers:
1. What it is about, in 2 lines.
2. What changes for our school, as bullet points.
3. Who must do what, by when, as a table: Action | Responsible role | Deadline.
4. Questions we should ask CBSE or our Sahodaya.
Use only what the circular says. If something is unclear, say "not stated in the circular".
Circular text: [paste the text, or upload the PDF]
OF-03 Staff meeting agenda and minutes · स्टाफ बैठक का एजेंडा और कार्यवृत्त
When: Before and after any staff, SMC or PTM meeting.
Prepare an agenda for a [45]-minute [staff / SMC / department] meeting on [date].
Topics: [topic 1], [topic 2], [topic 3]. Add time for each item and who leads it.
After the meeting I will paste my rough points. Then turn them into minutes with:
Decisions taken | Action | Owner (role) | Deadline. Keep it to one page.
OF-04 Reply to an official letter or email · आधिकारिक पत्र या ईमेल का उत्तर
When: A letter from the DIOS office, CBSE regional office, Sahodaya or a vendor needs a polite, clear reply.
Draft a formal reply to the letter below on behalf of the Principal of [school name].
Our position: [what we agree to / what we need / what we cannot do and why].
Tone: respectful, clear, short (under 150 words). Include subject line and reference to their letter number and date.
Letter: [paste here]
OF-05 Notice in simple Hindi for support staff · सहायक कर्मचारियों के लिए सरल हिंदी सूचना
When: Instructions for drivers, peons, guards or helpers who prefer Hindi.
Write a short notice in very simple Hindi (Devanagari) for our [drivers / support staff] about [topic, e.g. new bus timings, safety drill, uniform].
Use short sentences and numbered points. No more than 8 points. Add a line at the end: "कोई प्रश्न हो तो कार्यालय में [name/role] से मिलें।"

Teachers, CPD & observation · शिक्षक, CPD और अवलोकन

ST-01 Find CPD gaps and plan training · CPD की कमी पहचानें और प्रशिक्षण योजना बनाएँ
When: Every month, after updating the CPD Tracker. · Command Sheet: CPD Tracker · Practised in Session 1 · Mission 3
Below is my CPD Tracker (teacher codes only). The target is [50] hours per teacher per year, with at least [25] through CBSE or government institutes.
1. List teachers who are "Critical" or "Behind", with hours still needed.
2. Group them by subject and suggest a school-based workshop plan for the next [8] weeks to close the gap.
3. Suggest free CBSE/government options they could take (I will verify each one).
Data: [copy the CPD Tracker table from Teacher code to Status and paste here]
ST-02 Fair observation rubric · निष्पक्ष कक्षा-अवलोकन रूब्रिक
When: Before a round of classroom observations. · Command Sheet: Observation Log · Practised in Session 1 · Mission 4
Create a classroom observation rubric with 5 criteria: Planning & materials, Engagement, Questioning & checking, Inclusion, Link to computational thinking.
For each criterion describe levels 1 Beginning, 2 Developing, 3 Proficient, 4 Exemplary in one line each, as a table.
Make it observable in a [35]-minute visit, suitable for Classes [___], and usable without any device in the classroom.
ST-03 Kind, specific observation feedback · दयालु और विशिष्ट अवलोकन प्रतिक्रिया
When: After you have filled a row in the Observation Log. · Command Sheet: Observation Log · Practised in Session 1 · Mission 4
Here is one row from my Observation Log (teacher code only). Write feedback for the teacher that:
- starts with two specific strengths I saw,
- gives no more than two next steps, each with one practical classroom idea,
- offers the support I will provide,
- is warm and respectful, under 150 words, in [English / Hindi / both].
Row: [paste the row with headers]
ST-04 Spot patterns across many observations · कई अवलोकनों में पैटर्न पहचानें
When: At the end of a month or term, to plan whole-staff training. · Command Sheet: Observation Log
Below is my Observation Log for [month/term] (teacher codes only).
1. Which criterion has the lowest average across the school? Which has the highest?
2. Name 3 patterns you see, with evidence from the rows.
3. Suggest one 30-minute staff workshop for the weakest area, with an agenda.
Do not rank or label individual teachers. Data: [paste the table]
ST-05 Substitution and duty roster · प्रतिस्थापन और ड्यूटी रोस्टर
When: When several teachers are absent or for exam/assembly duties.
Make a substitution plan for today. Absent: [teacher codes and their periods]. Free teachers by period: [list].
Rules: no teacher gets more than [2] extra periods, prefer same-subject substitutes, keep Class X and XII covered first.
Give a table: Period | Class | Absent teacher | Substitute | Suggested activity.
ST-06 Teacher appreciation note · शिक्षक प्रशंसा पत्र
When: To recognise good work at an assembly, in a staff group or a letter.
Write a short appreciation note (under 90 words) for a teacher who [what they did, e.g. ran the science fair, improved Class 8 results]. Make it specific and sincere, not flowery. Give [English / Hindi / both].

Below is my CT & AI Plan (classes, sections, hours, periods per week). Our timetable has [8] periods a day, [6] days a week.
Suggest where these periods can come from without dropping any board subject: which existing periods to integrate CT into, and which to use as dedicated CT & AI periods.
Give a table per class: Day | Period | Subject period used | CT & AI activity type. Flag any class where the plan is not realistic.
Plan: [copy the CT & AI Plan table and paste here]
CU-02 Unplugged CT lesson (no lab) · बिना कंप्यूटर CT पाठ
When: A teacher needs a CT lesson that works with chalk, cards or objects. · Practised in Session 3 · Mission 3
Design a [35]-minute unplugged computational-thinking lesson for Class [___] on [pattern recognition / algorithms / decomposition / abstraction].
Use only things a village classroom has: chalk, paper, bottle caps, bangles, the children themselves.
Include: learning objective, a 5-minute hook, the main activity in steps, 3 check questions, and a 2-line homework task. Link it to the CBSE CT & AI handbook for that class where possible (I will verify).
CU-03 Integrate CT into a subject chapter · विषय के अध्याय में CT जोड़ें
When: To show teachers that CT is not an extra subject.
For Class [___] [subject], chapter "[chapter name]", suggest 3 short activities (5–10 minutes each) that teach computational thinking while teaching the chapter.
For each: CT skill used, steps, materials, and one question to check understanding. Keep it within the NCERT textbook content.
CU-04 Student AI project on a local problem · स्थानीय समस्या पर छात्र AI परियोजना
When: For a Class VI–IX project, science fair or ATL activity. · Practised in Session 3 · Mission 6
Suggest 5 low-cost student AI project ideas for Classes [___] in a [rural / small-town] school in [district], UP, that solve a local problem (water, farming, traffic, waste, health, attendance).
For each: the problem, what the students build (e.g. a Teachable Machine model), data they can collect safely without personal information, materials, and a 4-week plan.
CU-05 Annual academic calendar draft · वार्षिक शैक्षणिक कैलेंडर का मसौदा
When: At the start of the session or before the second term.
Draft an academic calendar for [April 2027–March 2028] for a CBSE school in Uttar Pradesh.
Include: term dates, periodic tests, half-yearly and annual exams, PTMs, CT & AI project weeks, sports day, annual day, and UP public holidays (mark these "verify against the official list").
Give a month-by-month table. Leave the two Class X board exam windows marked "as per CBSE date sheet".

Teaching & assessment · शिक्षण और मूल्यांकन

AS-01 Competency-based questions, board pattern · बोर्ड पैटर्न की योग्यता-आधारित प्रश्न
When: Building a question bank or a periodic test. · Command Sheet: Question Bank · Practised in Session 3 · Mission 4
Create [5] competency-based questions for Class [___] [subject], chapter "[chapter]":
[2] assertion-reason, [2] case-based, [1] source-based. Follow the latest CBSE sample-paper style.
Give the output as a table with exactly these columns so I can paste it into my sheet:
ID | Class | Subject | Chapter | Question type | Question | Answer | Marks | Competency tested
Use only content from the NCERT textbook.
AS-02 Make AI check its own questions · AI से अपने प्रश्न स्वयं जँचवाएँ
When: Right after AS-01, before any teacher review. · Command Sheet: Question Bank · Practised in Session 3 · Mission 4
Now act as a strict CBSE paper-setter and check each question you just wrote:
- Is the answer correct and complete? Is the question within the NCERT chapter?
- Is there more than one correct option or any ambiguity?
- Does it test application, not just recall?
Give a table: ID | Problem found | Corrected version. If a question is fine, write "No change".
AS-03 Keep answers inside the textbook (NotebookLM) · उत्तर पाठ्यपुस्तक के भीतर रखें (NotebookLM)
When: Use in NotebookLM after uploading the NCERT chapter PDF. · Practised in Session 3 · Mission 5
Using only the uploaded chapter, create a one-page revision note for Class [___] students with: 8 key ideas, 5 important terms with meanings, and 5 practice questions with page references.
If something is not in the chapter, do not add it.
AS-04 Remedial plan from performance bands · प्रदर्शन बैंड से उपचारात्मक योजना
When: After a test, using bands (Strong / Developing / Needs support), not marks or names.
In Class [___] [subject], after the [test name]: [number] students are Strong, [number] Developing, [number] Need support. The weakest topics were [topics].
Plan 4 weeks of remedial support that fits inside normal periods: weekly focus, a 10-minute daily routine, peer-learning ideas, and a short check at the end of each week.
AS-05 Lesson plan with differentiation · विविधता के साथ पाठ योजना
When: For a teacher who wants a ready lesson plan that reaches every learner.
Write a [40]-minute lesson plan for Class [___] [subject] on "[topic]".
Include objectives, a hook, main activity, three levels of tasks (support / core / challenge), a quick exit check, and homework. Mention one way to include a child with [reading difficulty / low vision / hearing difficulty].
